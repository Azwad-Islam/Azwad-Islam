### 🔬 Research

<table>
<tr><td>

**🎙️ Advancing Emotion Detection in Audio using a Transformer-based Hybrid BiLSTM Model**
<br/><sub>B.Sc. thesis · AIUB · October 2025 · team of 4</sub>

A speech emotion recognition model, **HybridLSTMTransformer**, that combines stacked **BiLSTMs** for local temporal patterns with **Transformer encoders** for long-range context. Attention pooling and fusion of **prosodic features** (pitch, energy) with **MFCCs** sharpen subtle emotional distinctions.

- 🎯 **79% validation accuracy** on RAVDESS (1,344 samples, 7 emotions)
- 📈 **+7.25%** over a CNN baseline and **+10.8%** over an LSTM baseline
- 🧪 Ablation studies with speaker-independent evaluation
- 🛠️ PyTorch · Librosa · scikit-learn

</td></tr>
